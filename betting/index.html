Long-shot builder

Builds multi-match bet builders at a target price, one builder per match, combined as an accumulator. Within each match the legs are priced from the score grid, so correlation is built in. Across matches the games are treated as independent. A 100/1 slip at fair odds wins about 1 time in 100. bet365 builder prices carry a bigger margin than single markets, and the margin multiplies across matches. A slip is only value if the bet365 price you are offered is above the fair price shown.

"Model v market" compares the model's chance with the chance implied by the market-only goal rates. Above 1 means group form makes the slip more likely than the market thinks. Gaps compound across matches, and they come from only three group games, so a figure well above 1 is mostly noise rather than a real edge. The steady style uses only legs where the two agree within about 5%, and spreads the price across matches so no single leg is a long shot.

How the numbers are made

  1. Market rates. The 1X2 prices are de-margined with the power method. The model then searches for the home and away goal rates whose score grid reproduces those win, draw and loss chances, plus over 2.5 where a price exists. The fit error shown on each match is the root-mean-square gap.
  2. Form rates. Attack and defence ratings come from goals for and against in this campaign's group games, shrunk toward the group average by 3 games' worth of data. The home side gets a 1.12 goal multiplier (none at neutral venues).
  3. Blend. Goal rates are combined on a log scale, 75% market and 25% form. Belarus v Finland has no reliable price, so it uses form only.
  4. Score grid. Poisson for each side with the Dixon-Coles correction (rho -0.06) on 0-0, 1-0, 0-1 and 1-1, scores up to 10-10. Every full-time market and every bet builder leg is summed from this one grid, so correlated legs combine exactly.
  5. Halves. 44% of each side's expected goals are placed in the first half, with the two halves independent.
  6. Value. EV = p × (odds − 1) − (1 − p − push). Asian lines refund pushes. Matches flagged low confidence carry wider error.
